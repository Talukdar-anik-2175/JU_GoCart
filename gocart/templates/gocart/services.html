Empowering Green Mobility at JU

At Jahangirnagar University, the Electric Vehicle (EV) initiative represents a visionary shift towards sustainable campus living. Supported by RideSwift’s smart booking solutions, JU’s EV service is redefining convenience while championing environmental stewardship. Together, we are driving forward a future where innovation meets responsibility — one ride at a time.
